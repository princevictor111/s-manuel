    About S'Manuel Consulting

    S'Manuel Consulting is a growing HR outsourcing and business consulting company dedicated to helping businesses streamline their human resource processes, improve operational efficiency, and drive organizational success. Established in 2018, we specialize in HR consulting, business management, training and development, process improvement, LinkedIn optimization, CV and personal statement writing, and a variety of other business support services.

    With a strong presence in the UK and Lagos - Nigeria, we have worked with numerous clients across diverse industries globally, offering tailored HR solutions and strategic business insights to help companies attract, retain, and develop top talent while ensuring compliance and efficiency in their operations.
